About

Hello, I'm Bar.

I'm an Israeli game designer with a generalist background, studying Independent Game Production at DAE in Belgium.

I enjoy coming up with ideas within constraints, finding new and effective solutions to design problems, and playtesting: watching how players react to what I made, whether or not it's what I expected.

Games have been one of my favourite pastimes since I was a kid. They're the most immersive medium we have, and they work as art, as a way to learn, and as a reason to get friends together. In the future I want to create new kinds of games and show what games can do as art and as tools that help people.

Away from the screen Reading novels and manga · Travelling and hiking · Board games, the more players the better · Trading card games
