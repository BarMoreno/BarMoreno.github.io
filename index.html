About

Hello, I'm Bar.

I'm a generalist game designer studying Independent Game Production at DAE in Belgium.

I want to find the fun in every game, and to build kinds of experiences that haven't been made before. Most of my projects start from one small mechanic that I iterate on until it feels right, and I like doing the art for my own prototypes too.
